Qualitative comparison of the final configuration on VisDrone DET test-dev. Columns show ground truth, YOLO-1280, HyCADO-1280, and SAHI-640. The three main images are the first three entries of a fixed SHA256-ranked sample with distinct filename-based proxy groups; predictions and method performance do not enter image selection. The full twelve-image sample is provided as supplementary material. Each row shows the same native-resolution window for all methods. A 640×420-pixel window is selected by the highest normal-GT center count among at most 100 annotation-order candidate centers, with deterministic coordinate tie-breaking; this GT-based presentation choice is not part of inference. Archived final predictions are shown at a common numerical score threshold of 0.05 after the same pinned ignore-region preprocessing as the quantitative evaluation. Scores are not recalibrated, and this threshold does not imply matched false-positive rates. Colors denote object classes, not true/false-positive status. Boxes crossing a window boundary are clipped for display only. These examples illustrate local detection patterns rather than establish aggregate superiority.
