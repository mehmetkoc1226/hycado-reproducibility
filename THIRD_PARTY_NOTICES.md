# Third-party components

The historical code imports Ultralytics, PyTorch, torchvision, NumPy, OpenCV, Pillow, PyYAML and SciPy. These dependencies are not vendored here and retain their respective licenses. The pinned implementation is Ultralytics 8.3.203; a new project-wide license grant is not added by this preparation bundle.

VisDrone images/annotations must be obtained from their maintainers under the applicable dataset terms. The public evaluator is identified by commit `005445782213e20cb91bc50a597db3dd949e749a` in https://github.com/VisDrone/VisDrone2018-DET-toolkit. Its source is referenced, not copied into this repository. The GNU Octave compatibility execution is a recorded verification step, not a bundled MATLAB license or runtime.

The paper source and this preparation package do not assign rights to external pretrained weights, datasets, software or publications.
